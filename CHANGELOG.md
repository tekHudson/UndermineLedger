# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Initial release. Tracks Tarnished Undermine Real loot per boss across all
  SoD dungeons (BRD, Blackrock Spire [LBRS+UBRS], Scholomance, Stratholme,
  Dire Maul, Demon Fall Canyon, Karazhan Crypts) and raids (Molten Core,
  Blackwing Lair, Zul'Gurub, AQ20, AQ40, Onyxia's Lair), plus passive
  tracking of open-world uniques (Azuregos, Lord Kazzak, Prince Thunderaan,
  the four Dragons of Nightmare).
- Zone-entry alert icon with a red/green boss list popup.
- Ledger summary window across all instances.
- Self-learning boss roster.
